# Lane D status: city + simulator

- Works: `POST /api/simulate` returns a real `Prediction` in about 0.4 to 0.7 s for 20 runs. `GET /api/city/restaurants` serves 192 real places, and `GET /api/city/residents?sample=N` serves 10,000 seeded residents. `bun server/src/sim/sanity.ts` passes all 26 checks. If the simulator throws, the route serves the fixture and says so in `calibration.note`.
- Calibrated (brief D2, part 2): hour curves, day factors and cuisine time-of-day taste are fitted to Google popular times. On 13 held-out places the median correlation is 0.54. The naive city-average curve scores 0.44, and the hand-set model scored 0.32. Every prediction carries 0.54 in `calibration.heldOutCorrelation`. Method, chart and limits: `docs/calibration.md`. Rerun: `bun scripts/calibrate/calibrate.ts`.
- Existing restaurants (brief D2, part 1): with `concept.existing.placeId`, the concept replaces that real place. It keeps the real rating, reviews and regulars, and `stolenFrom` is measured against today's city. The prediction adds `baselineOrders`, `observedByHour` and `observedCorrelation`. An unchanged import lands on its orders today (CHiKN 68 vs 66, Noodlehead 47 vs 48).
- Faked: the population is a hand-set mix per area, not Census data, and `tractId` holds zone ids such as `zone:shadyside`. Competitor prices come from the price tier, and competitor diet options from cuisine odds. Google shows foot traffic, not delivery.
- For other lanes: the fixture concept now gets about 57 Friday orders (67 before calibration), so a 120-order optimize goal is further away. `explainResident()` takes an optional hour. For an existing place its factor reads "4.3 stars here vs X", not "new place vs X" (Lane F's regex). Optional contract fields from Lane D: `avgTicketBand`, `marketSharePctBand`, `sampleDecisions`, `baselineOrders`.
