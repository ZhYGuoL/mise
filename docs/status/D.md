# Lane D status: city + simulator

- Works: `POST /api/simulate` returns a real `Prediction` in about 250 ms (20 runs, 1.1 s at 100 runs). `GET /api/city/restaurants` serves 192 real places, and `GET /api/city/residents?sample=N` serves 10,000 seeded residents. `bun server/src/sim/sanity.ts` passes all 11 checks (price, vegan main, location, fees, hours, day, launch promo, identical output for a tagline edit).
- Faked: the residents use a hand-set segment mix per area, not Census data. `tractId` holds zone ids such as `zone:shadyside`, not real tracts. Competitor menu prices come from the price tier, and competitor diet options are drawn from cuisine odds. Hours come from Google popular times, or from cuisine defaults when a place has none. The model is not calibrated: `calibration.note` is "not calibrated yet".
- For Wave 2: `explainResident(concept, scenario, id)` in `server/src/sim/explain.ts` returns the real utility terms (`breakdown`, `choseInstead`, `wouldOrderFromConcept`). `Prediction.sampleDecisions` holds that decision for each sampled resident, and the resident list in `web/src/right/panels/Breakdown.tsx` shows it, so the list and the interview agree. The fixture concept gets about 67 orders on a Friday (p10 to p90 is 60 to 102). A 20% launch promo moves it to about 110.
- Rebuild the city with `bun server/src/city/build.ts`. The output is fixed by its seed. If the simulator throws, the route serves the fixture and sets `calibration.note` to "simulation failed, fixture data". Added to `shared/contracts.ts` (optional): `Prediction.avgTicketBand`, `Prediction.marketSharePctBand` and `Prediction.sampleDecisions`.
