# Lane D: City + simulator

Read `CLAUDE.md` and `shared/contracts.ts` first. Branch `lane/d-sim`.

## You own
`server/src/routes/{simulate,city}.ts`, `server/src/sim/**`, `server/src/city/**`, `data/city/**`.

## Goal
`POST /api/simulate {concept, scenario}` -> `Prediction` that feels real in a demo: numbers move the
right way when the owner changes price, hours, location or menu, and the map animation looks like a
living city. Product first. Rigor (census sourcing, calibration) is Phase 3 and only if time allows.

## Phase 1 (by ~12:45): the city, quick and plausible
- `data/city/restaurants.json` (contract `Restaurant[]`) from `data/raw/` (79 real restaurants with
  ratings, review counts and popular times, plus the 192 OpenStreetMap ones for map dots). Map cuisines
  to a small vocabulary. Guess missing price tiers from the category.
- `data/city/residents.json`: ~10,000 residents generated by code with a fixed seed. Hand-set mix per
  area is fine for now: Oakland mostly undergrads and grad students, Shadyside young professionals,
  Squirrel Hill North families, East Liberty mixed. Weights drawn per segment (students care about price,
  families about time and portions). Personas from a few templates (no LLM needed yet).
- `GET /api/city/restaurants`, `GET /api/city/residents?sample=N`.

## Phase 2 (by the 2:00 merge): the simulator
- Per resident per hour: (A) order now? from orders-per-week, a segment hour curve, weather, event.
  (B) from where? filter by open, within ~3 km, diet compatible; score by price vs budget, delivery
  time, cuisine taste, rating, favorites bonus, unknown-new-place penalty, promos; pick by softmax.
- Never call the LLM per decision.
- 20 runs -> p10/p50/p90 bands. Baseline run without the concept -> `stolenFrom`. Biggest negative
  term among near-misses -> `objections` with plain labels. Sample up to 600 orders -> `orderFlows`.
- Under ~3 s per call. Precompute distances once.
- Sanity check by hand: cheaper -> more undergrad orders; add a vegan main -> fewer diet objections;
  move far from campus -> fewer orders. If one of these goes the wrong way, fix it before anything else.

## Phase 3 (only after 3:30 and only if the product is solid)
- Swap the hand-set population for US Census ACS tract data.
- Fit hour curves to the Google popular-times shapes (`data/raw/popular_times_long.csv`, relative to
  each place's own peak, foot traffic not delivery), hold out 20%, put the correlation in
  `prediction.calibration`. Until then `calibration.note` says "not calibrated yet".

## Done when (Phase 2)
Real Predictions for `data/fixtures/concept.json` in under 3 s, the three sanity checks hold, and
`docs/status/D.md` has 3-5 lines.
